About

I'm Abubakar Albakri, though almost everyone calls me Akira. I came to computer science the long way round — I spent a year on a biomedical sciences degree at Sheffield before admitting that the part of it I actually enjoyed was the data analysis, and started again at Manchester Metropolitan.

It turned out to be the right call. I'm now in my final year with first class results in seven of nine modules, including algorithms and data structures, databases, and web development. The lab-work habits carried over better than I expected — writing things down properly, changing one variable at a time, not trusting a result you can't reproduce.

Alongside the degree I've taken three Microsoft Azure certifications, and I hold down two part-time roles — IT and customer support at a training centre, and cleaning work on commercial sites. That leaves a finite number of hours for everything else, which is probably why I build the things I do: every project on this page removes a task somebody was doing manually, including me.

Outside of it I played basketball through school and still do when I can. I'm currently working through Spring Boot and Docker, and I'm looking for a placement where I'd be writing software with people who are better at it than I am.
